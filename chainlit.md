# Nexora AI

Nexora AI is a chat assistant that can:

- **Chat** with Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI or a local Ollama model (pick one in the settings panel).
- **Answer from your documents.** Attach PDF, text, Markdown, CSV or JSON files to a message. Answers cite the passages they used.
- **Build software with a MetaGPT team.** `/build <idea>` asks a product manager, architect, project manager and engineer to write requirements, a design and code. You get a zip to review. Nothing is executed.

Type `/help` for all commands.

AI can make mistakes. Check important information, and don't upload confidential data.
